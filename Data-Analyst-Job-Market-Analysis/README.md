# 📊 Data Analyst Job Market Analysis

An exploratory analysis of the Indian Data Analyst job market using Python, Pandas, and Power BI.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes job postings from the Indian technology job market to identify trends in job demand, required skills, salary ranges, experience levels, work modes, and fresher opportunities.

The original dataset contained **23,201 job postings**, from which **4,729 Data Analyst-related jobs** were selected for detailed analysis.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze Data Analyst job demand across Indian cities
- Identify the most in-demand skills
- Analyze salary distribution and salary tiers
- Compare average salaries across experience levels
- Identify cities with more fresher-friendly opportunities
- Analyze On-site, Remote, and Hybrid work opportunities
- Identify companies hiring for Data Analyst roles
- Explore the relationship between skill demand and average salary
- Analyze salary disclosure trends

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **4,729** Data Analyst-related jobs were identified.
- **1,287** jobs were classified as fresher-friendly.
- The average disclosed salary was approximately **10.62 LPA**.
- **Data Analysis, SQL, Power BI, Python, and Business Analysis** were among the frequently mentioned skills.
- Most jobs in the dataset were **On-site**.
- A significant share of job postings had salary information undisclosed.
